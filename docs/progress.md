## Model ordering
The managed model selector groups OpenRouter models with explicit zero input and output token prices first, then unknown-price models, then models with a positive input/output price. Other providers report unknown pricing because their model-list responses do not establish account-specific free tiers. Among free models with explicit simple parameter counts in their IDs, larger counts appear first; unknown sizes follow. This is only a size heuristic, not a benchmark or guaranteed quality ranking, and MoE size patterns are not guessed. Zero token pricing does not rule out other charges or quota limits. No real provider catalogue or price has been verified during this change. Existing credential and live-preview limitations remain.
